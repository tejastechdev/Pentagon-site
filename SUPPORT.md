# Pentagon — Support

Pentagon is an arcade defence game by ThoughtOcean Technologies: your pentagon spins in the middle, enemies close in from every side, and you spend coins mid-run on upgrades while building a permanent loadout of guns, bullets and bases between runs.

## How to play

- **Play** starts a run. The pentagon fires automatically at the nearest enemy.
- Tap **UPGRADES** at the bottom during a run to spend coins on attack power, attack speed, health and regen.
- Skill buttons on the edge of the screen fire your equipped abilities (they show a cooldown dial).
- When your HP reaches zero the run ends and you earn **Ducks** based on the coins you collected and the Bigs you destroyed.
- **Turret**: equip a Gun, Bullet and Base, pick two skills, and shape your shot sound.
- **Store**: open chests with Ducks for new parts, buy colour schemes with Diamonds, and redeem codes.

## Enemies

| Enemy | Notes |
|---|---|
| Classic | 1 damage, 1 HP, 1 coin |
| Big | 2 damage, 3 HP, 5 coins, knocked back when hit |
| Skelly | Harmless, 10 coins, only destroyed by a Bomb's explosion, always arrives with a Bomb |
| Bomb | 3 damage, 1 HP, 3 coins, explodes when destroyed |

## Frequently asked

**My progress disappeared.** Progress is saved only on the device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

**The music stopped.** Check the MUSIC / SOUND toggles on the Home screen, and the iPhone's silent switch.

**A code says "already redeemed".** Every code works once per device.
