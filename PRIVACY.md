# Pentagon — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 September 2026
**Developer:** ThoughtOcean Technologies

Pentagon is a single-player arcade game. We designed it to work without collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Pentagon does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The game does not connect to the internet.
- There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other device data.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (currencies, unlocked parts, settings) is saved **only on your device**, using the standard iOS app storage. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, and collects no data, and is suitable for all ages.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
